// what-it-looks-like

What sponsorship actually is.

Concrete things with a cost attached. Tell us which one you want to back and we will tell you what it takes.

01 / VENUE

Host a session

A room in Lahore, Karachi, or Islamabad with power, seating, and a screen. The most useful thing anyone can give us.

02 / HOSPITALITY

Feed the room

Chai and food for an evening session. Hospitality is not a nice-to-have here; it is why people stay for the second half.

03 / SPEAKER

Move a speaker

Travel or accommodation so a practitioner from another city can present in person rather than over a video call.

04 / LABS

Fund the labs

Cloud credits or cluster time for hands-on workshops, so attendees break things in a real environment instead of watching slides.

// terms

What sponsorship is not.

Response expectation: an organizer replies within 3 working days with a real answer — what we need, what it costs, and what we can credit. A conversation with a person, not a tier sheet and not a deck.

Pure logo-spam is not for sale. We will name you from the stage, in the session notes, and on the event page for what you actually backed. We will not turn a session into an advertising surface, and we will not invent a partnership that did not happen.

  • No attendee or member data. No lists, no emails, no scanned badges. Not for sponsors, not for anyone.
  • No vendor blasts. Sponsors do not get to message members. Sponsored sessions, yes; direct messages to the community, never.
  • No pitch dressed as a talk. Talk slots are decided by the programme committee on merit. Backing an event does not buy the microphone.
  • No paid membership tier. Membership is free. Sponsorship never buys standing inside the community.
  • Your address is used for this conversation only. Writing to us does not subscribe you to anything, and we add no one to a mailing list from this form.

Community members read this page too. If a sponsorship would cost us their trust, we turn it down. Everyone involved is bound by the Code of Conduct, and if you would rather take part than fund us, start at the join page.
