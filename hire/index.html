// how-it-works

How you post a role.

Four steps, all of them human. There is no job board to log into and no account to create.

01 / SEND

Send the role

Use the form below: who you are, the organisation, the role you are hiring for, and the detail a candidate needs — location, whether it is remote, and the compensation range.

02 / CHECK

We read it

An organizer reads every submission. We check the role is real, currently open, and named — no blind "confidential client" listings, no unpaid work presented as experience.

03 / POST

Posted once

The role goes out once in the community channel, credited to you. If a session is coming up and the role fits the room, an organizer mentions it there too.

04 / DIRECT

Candidates come to you

Interested members apply through the contact route in your posting. We do not sit in the middle, forward CVs, or take a fee.

// terms

What you get, and what we will not do.

Response expectation: an organizer replies within 3 working days. If the role is a fit we tell you when it goes out; if it is not, we say so and why. You will not be left waiting on a silent inbox.

  • No CV database. CNSPK does not sell, rent, or share a candidate list. There is nothing to buy access to.
  • No member email addresses. Member emails stay in the private registry. They are never exported to a hiring partner.
  • No bulk messages to members. We do not send direct messages on any channel on behalf of an employer.
  • Your address is used for this role only. Sending a role does not subscribe you to anything. We add no one to a mailing list from this form.
  • Recruiter spam is removed. Repeat blasts, fake roles, and pay-to-apply schemes get the posting pulled and the sender removed from the channels.

Looking to join the community rather than hire from it? Start at the join page. Everyone posting here is bound by the Code of Conduct.

Send us the role.

Five fields. The more concrete the role description, the better it lands with practitioners.

Format: local@domain. Used to reply to you about this role.

For example: Platform Security Engineer, Karachi, hybrid.

Scope, stack, seniority, location or remote policy, compensation range, and how candidates should reach you. Up to 1000 characters.
